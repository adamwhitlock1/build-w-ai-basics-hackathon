# Porchlight: naming round 1

2026-10-23. Why we're looking: a pending PORCHLIGHT application covers AI family-briefing software (see [`research.md`](research.md)). This round looks for names that keep the porch-light idea and gives each a first-pass screen.

**This is a screen, not a clearance.** It catches the obvious problems so an attorney's time goes to two or three names, not thirty. See "What this screen doesn't cover" at the end.

## Recommendation

1. **Kinlight** is the clear first choice. Take it to a trademark attorney for a full clearance search.
2. **Lamplit** and **Stoopside** are the backups, in that order.
3. Keep building the identity on the light, the porch and the steps. All three names carry it, and the wordmark's lit dot works in each (every one has an i).

## How each name carries the identity

- **The lit dot.** Kinlight, Lamplit and Stoopside each have an i (Porchly is the only finalist without one). Kinlight has two: lighting the second keeps "light" glowing, while lighting the first puts the glow on "kin."
- **The marks.** Concepts 2 (Light under the roof) and 5 (Haint ceiling) don't use a letter, so they carry over unchanged.
- **Voice.** "The light's on" still works as the line for all of them. Kinlight adds "Kinlight is on" as a natural phrase.

## Method

- **Trademarks:** queried the USPTO's own trademark search (tmsearch.uspto.gov) on 2026-10-23 for every live mark whose wordmark contains the name, its spaced form and its plurals or extensions (for example FRONTSTEP* caught FRONTSTEPS). Classes treated as relevant: 9 and 42 (software), plus 35, 38, 41 and 45 (adjacent services).
- **Domains:** .com through Verisign whois and RDAP, .app through Google Registry RDAP, .family through Identity Digital RDAP. "Free" means unregistered at the registry; registrars may still charge a premium price. For registered .com domains, looked at what the site serves and whether it's listed for sale.
- **Products:** web searches for apps and companies using each finalist.

## What this screen doesn't cover

- Marks that sound or look similar without sharing the word (KYNLITE, KINDLELIGHT), and design marks.
- Unregistered (common-law) use beyond a web search, state registrations, and business names.
- Other countries (EUIPO, UKIPO, WIPO), if a launch outside the US is planned.
- App Store and Google Play name availability.
- A legal opinion. An attorney's clearance search on Kinlight (and a backup) is the next step before buying the domain.
