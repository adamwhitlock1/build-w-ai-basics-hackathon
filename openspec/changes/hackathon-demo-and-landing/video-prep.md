# Kinlight video and submission preparation

Planning checklist, not a claim that any deployment, recording or submission has happened. The owner writes narration and personal submission answers. Implementation supplies the demo, technical instructions and recording setup.

## Confirm requirements — owner

Checked 2026-10-25 against the [official rules](https://learn-ai-basics.devpost.com/rules) and [overview](https://learn-ai-basics.devpost.com/). The submission deadline is October 26 at 5 p.m. Eastern / 3 p.m. Denver. Judging ends October 30 at 5 p.m. Eastern / 3 p.m. Denver. Recheck the live form before submitting.

- [ ] Confirm registration and actual required form/survey fields in the owner's account.
- [ ] Prepare a public repository with source, setup instructions and the historical scope/PRD/spec. Review its license and publication contents, including history, for private material.
- [ ] Aim for about two minutes: the overview requests 1–3 minutes; rules recommend under three. Show the working application.
- [ ] Upload the video publicly to YouTube or Vimeo and supply its link. Verify permissions, English narration/captions and rights to any included material.
- [ ] Supply demo parent/kid credentials in testing instructions and keep access working through judging. Do not require approval emails or the owner's Cloudflare login.

## Prepare the experience — implementer, then owner verifies

- [ ] Deploy the dedicated demo; confirm its database, volume, port and keys differ from the real family's.
- [ ] Reset prepared content before rehearsal; ensure quota remains adequate rather than assuming reset replenishes it.
- [ ] Inspect all four family pages. Confirm populated, readable cards; current calendar examples; varied daily chores; sample chats and a review item.
- [ ] Use two browser profiles/windows: Maya for parent actions and Jordan for child actions. Confirm actual child login rather than only switching the parent view.
- [ ] Rehearse one widget prompt and one homework interaction with live AI. Pick examples that work, without promising identical model output on every run.
- [ ] Check one parent review and one chore check/undo. Reset recording state after rehearsal where needed.
- [ ] Capture a screenshot using only fictional content for the landing page. Verify its crop at phone and desktop widths.

## Recording setup — owner

- [ ] Close unrelated tabs and notifications; hide password managers, terminal secrets, real chats and bookmarks containing personal details.
- [ ] Use a clean 16:9 capture with readable browser zoom. Check microphone level and play back a short test.
- [ ] Keep login credentials offscreen; sign into the two roles ahead of time.
- [ ] Write narration in your own words: the family problem, who benefits, what makes this useful, and one concrete lesson from building it.
- [ ] Rehearse once with a timer. Spend most of the recording showing actions and results.

## Suggested shot sequence — owner records

| Approximate time | Screen/action | Evidence to capture |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:15 | Landing page, then populated parent homepage | Audience and daily family context are immediately understandable |
| 0:15–0:45 | Describe one widget, wait for real result, arrange and save | The original describe-to-interface idea works end to end |
| 0:45–1:10 | Parent assigns a chore; switch browser to child and complete it | A real shared-family workflow, with clear roles |
| 1:10–1:40 | Child asks a homework question; parent opens the prepared review example | Live help and parent oversight; identify the review as seeded sample content |
| 1:40–2:00 | Return to the finished dashboard | Owner's reflection and where judges can try the project |

Do not rush a real AI response to fit a timestamp. Trim transitions or extend within the limit. If a wait is edited out, make that clear; do not present a seeded conversation as a live model response. If the landing page is not ready, open on the working dashboard so it does not delay recording.

## Final access and submission check — owner

- [ ] Play the complete uploaded video in a signed-out browser; check clarity, duration, sound and privacy.
- [ ] Open the public repository signed out; confirm current setup instructions and required planning documents are accessible.
- [ ] Follow the exact demo testing instructions in a fresh browser, using only the supplied URL and credentials. Test both roles and one live AI action.
- [ ] Verify the landing demo/video links; do not publish a placeholder video link.
- [ ] Review allowance remaining after recording; ensure judges can complete the advertised flow and know how to report access trouble.
- [ ] Write the final submission description and learning reflection; complete any required exit survey and attach repository, video and demo/testing details in the form's appropriate fields.
- [ ] Submit before the deadline and save the confirmation and submitted URLs privately. Record the deployed revision; keep it available through judging.

## Deadline fallback

Prioritize the working demo, recording and public repository. Defer extra landing decoration, animations and additional screenshots. If hosted demo access cannot be made reliable, provide honest local run instructions and the working video, and remove claims or links implying a working hosted judge environment. Do not weaken production safety or touch the real family database to save time.
