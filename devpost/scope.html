Porchlight

A per-family-member homepage where every widget is AI-driven — pre-built ones are auto-curated from each member's interests, and any member can describe a new widget in plain language and have it built and rendered.

The Unique Kernel

Widgets that are alive, not hardcoded

The widgets on the page are not static UI components. They are AI-driven artifacts: the pre-built ones (articles, local events) are curated per-user by an LLM against that member's interests, and the user can create a new widget just by typing a sentence — the LLM picks from a small known menu of data sources, persists the spec, and the widget appears, populated. The "describe it in plain language and watch it appear" beat is what makes this not just a feed reader.

Amber path = the kernel: a sentence in, a working widget out. The pre-built widgets are the same AI Layer at work, just with a fixed shape.

What "Working" Looks Like

Three beats in 60 seconds

The demo video proves the kernel in under a minute:

1

Setup

A parent signs in, creates the family, adds the spouse and two kids, and sets interests for one of them (e.g., "space exploration, soccer, indie music").

2

Customize

That family member opens their homepage, sees the curated articles and local events widgets, and drags them into a new order. The layout persists.

3

Describe-a-widget

They type a sentence — "show me upcoming SpaceX launches" — and a new widget appears on the page, populated with live data.
